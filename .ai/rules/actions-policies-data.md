# Actions Policies Data

## Preserve hierarchical evaluation authority and history
Initial accomplishment evaluation belongs to the closest active Executive authority above the owning organizational unit. A University-root Executive may append a Presidential agreement or override only after an accepted subordinate Executive evaluation; never update the earlier row. Official/effective rating prefers the current submission's Presidential accepted evaluation, then its subordinate Executive accepted evaluation.
